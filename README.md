A 100-word monologue (exactly 100 words, 519 characters) for Vesskar, who drowned
three harvests and plans to unhook the dawn bell so no morning ever follows.
His tiredness is never announced — it lives in the tilting room, the forgotten
name written on his gauntlet, the guttering torches, and a final line that hands
his own doom to the hero.

Run: `python3 main.py` — writes the monologue byte-exact to `/output/entry.txt`.
